# Mouse Treatment

## Identification
House mice: small droppings (rice-grain size, pointed ends), gnaw marks with fine tooth patterns, grease rub marks along baseboards, nesting material (shredded paper/insulation), and scratching sounds in walls or attic at night. Droppings concentrate along runways and in kitchen drawers, under sinks, and in garages.

## Approach
Exterior baiting plus exclusion. Stations knock the outside population down; exclusion keeps new mice out. Interior work relies on trapping and monitoring — never place loose bait indoors. Interior infestation inspections are free for Plus customers; otherwise $175 during business hours / $300 after hours (per current program terms — confirm with the office).

## Products
- **Protecta Evo Express stations** — child- and pet-resistant exterior bait stations. Station counts by perimeter linear footage:
  - 200 linear ft or less: 6 stations (5 Evo Express, 1 Evo Landscape)
  - 200–300 linear ft: 8 stations (6 Evo Express, 2 Evo Landscape)
  - 300–400 linear ft: 12 stations (8 Evo Express, 4 Evo Landscape)
  - Over 400 linear ft: discuss with the Service Manager.
  Protecta Evo Landscape (rock-look) and Evo Circuit (cable-box look, good along walkways) can substitute where appearance or trip hazards matter. Placement in conducive areas beats even spacing.
- **Fasttrac Blox** rodenticide — 4 blox per station initially; maintain at least 2 per station for light activity, up to 8 if bait is fully consumed. Works after one feeding; rodents die in 5–7 days. Use ONLY inside secured Protecta stations, per label.
- Interior: snap traps and monitoring stations per label instructions — placement along walls and runways.
- Exclusion materials: door sweeps, copper mesh, sealant for gaps over 1/4 inch — per job scope.

## Treatment Steps
1. Inspect interior evidence areas (kitchen, garage, attic) and map runways with droppings and rub marks.
2. Walk the exterior and mark entry points: gaps under doors, utility penetrations, missing vent screens.
3. Place Protecta Evo stations by the linear-footage table, loaded with 4 Fasttrac Blox each.
4. Set interior snap traps on runways per label; no loose bait indoors.
5. Quote exclusion work for the entry points found (Plus customers get 30% off rodent service calls and exclusion work).
6. Photograph each station location for the service record.

## Customer Prep
- Store pantry food and pet food in sealed containers.
- Clear clutter from garage floors and under sinks so traps can go on runways.
- Don't move or open the exterior stations — they're locked for child and pet safety.

## Follow-up
- Recheck stations and traps in 1–2 weeks; replace bait that is consumed, moldy, or deteriorated.
- Replace any broken or unsecured station immediately — never leave bait unsecured.
- Move to routine station maintenance on the regular service schedule once activity stops.

## Safety
- Fasttrac Blox goes only in secured, labeled stations — follow all label directions for placement.
- Keep stations out of reach of children and pets even though they are resistant by design.
- Wear gloves when handling droppings; ventilate enclosed spaces before working in them.
