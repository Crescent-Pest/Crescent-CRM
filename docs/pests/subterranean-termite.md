# Subterranean Termite Treatment

## Identification
Eastern subterranean termites are the major wood-destroying pest in the Lowcountry. Evidence: mud tubes on foundations and piers, damaged wood that sounds hollow or shows layered galleries with soil, discarded wings near windows after spring swarms, and wood rot or damaged sill plates found during inspection. Swarmers are dark with equal-length wings (ants have elbowed antennae and unequal wings).

## Approach
Wood rot, mud tubes, or damaged sill plates mean a termite job, not a general pest treatment. Verify activity first, then choose liquid treatment or baiting based on construction and customer preference. Termite work is quoted as its own service.

## Products
- **Termidor HE** (fipronil termiticide, non-repellent) at 1.6 fl oz per gallon — liquid trench-and-treat around the foundation. Follow the label and South Carolina regulations for volume per linear foot.
- **Sentricon Recruit HD** — termite baiting system, one station every 10 ft around the structure. Placement rate, not a dilution.

## Treatment Steps
1. Full structure inspection: foundation walls, crawlspace, piers, sill plates, plumbing penetrations, and exterior grade line.
2. Document all evidence with photos: tubes, damage, moisture conditions.
3. Confirm live activity where possible (break a tube section and recheck).
4. Liquid option: trench and treat the foundation perimeter with Termidor HE at the label rate and volume; drill slabs/porches where the label requires continuous coverage.
5. Bait option: install Sentricon Recruit HD stations every 10 ft around the structure; log station locations.
6. Note conducive conditions for the customer: wood-to-ground contact, moisture, grade issues, stored wood against the house.

## Customer Prep
- Clear access to the crawlspace and foundation perimeter.
- Move stored items, firewood, and debris away from exterior walls.
- Plan to correct moisture and wood-contact issues — treatment works best with them fixed.

## Follow-up
- Liquid: annual re-inspection of the treated structure.
- Bait: station checks on the program schedule per label.
- Report any new tubes or swarmers immediately — covered re-treatment per agreement terms.

## Safety
- Termidor HE is a non-repellent — use dedicated non-repellent equipment.
- Follow label and state regulations exactly for application volumes and sites.
- Wear label-required PPE; keep people and pets clear during application.
