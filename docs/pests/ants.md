# Household Ant Treatment (Argentine & Odorous House Ants)

## Identification
Common Charleston-area household ants: Argentine ants (thin trails along edges), odorous house ants (rotten-coconut smell when crushed), big-headed ants, and acrobat ants. Evidence: visible trails along foundations and kitchen counters, nest mounds in mulch beds, ants following utility lines into the home. Fire ants and carpenter ants are excluded from the standard programs and have their own procedures — see the fire-ants and carpenter-ants documents.

## Approach
Non-repellent first. Ants cannot detect a non-repellent, so they walk through it and carry it back to the colony. Repellent sprays over ant trails just split the colony and make things worse.

## Products
- **Taurus SC** (fipronil, non-repellent) at 0.8 fl oz per gallon — the primary exterior ant product. Label restrictions you must follow: maximum 2 applications per year, and no application further than 1 ft from the structure. Apply with the dedicated non-repellent backpack sprayer.
- **Fipronil Plus-C** at 0.75 fl oz per gallon — exterior SPOT treatment for ant service calls. Spot treatment only, not a full perimeter product. Non-repellent.
- **Niban granular bait** — conducive areas per label (about 0.5 lb per home, roughly 4 ft band). Only where no spray went down.
- Interior: prefer monitors and baits per label instructions; treat verified trails at entry points only.

## Treatment Steps
1. Trail the ants: follow the line from where the customer sees them back to the entry point and, if possible, the nest.
2. Treat the exterior perimeter with Taurus SC (1 ft up, 1 ft out, per label) using the dedicated non-repellent sprayer.
3. Spot-treat trails and nest sites outside with Fipronil Plus-C.
4. Apply Niban in conducive areas away from sprayed zones.
5. Indoors, place ant bait on active trails per label; do not spray over trails.
6. Seal or note obvious entry gaps for the customer.

## Customer Prep
- Wipe up (don't spray) visible indoor trails before the visit — household sprays contaminate our treatment.
- Trim shrubs and trees off the structure (branches are ant highways).
- Keep sweets and pet food sealed for two weeks after treatment.

## Follow-up
- Expect MORE ant activity for a few days as the colony contacts the non-repellent — this is normal and means it's working.
- Recheck in 2 weeks if trails persist; free re-service between visits per program terms.

## Safety
- Never apply a repellent over Taurus SC or Fipronil Plus-C — it defeats the transfer effect.
- Respect the Taurus SC label: 2 applications per year maximum, within 1 ft of the structure.
- Wear label-required PPE.
