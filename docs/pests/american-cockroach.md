# American Cockroach (Palmetto Bug) Treatment

## Identification
Large (1.5–2 inch) reddish-brown roach — the Charleston "palmetto bug." Lives outdoors and in crawlspaces, sewers, and attics; wanders indoors through gaps and drains. Evidence: large droppings with ridged sides, egg cases in protected spots, sightings at night in bathrooms and kitchens.

## Approach
Exterior-based control: cut off the population outside before it moves in. Interior work is limited to entry points and verified activity, per Crescent's Assessment Based Pest Management standard.

## Products
- **Seasonal rotation residual** for the exterior perimeter (apply per label, 1 ft up and 1 ft out):
  - Barricor at 0.5 fl oz per gallon (Jan/Feb and Nov/Dec rotation) — repellent.
  - Onslaught FastCap at 1.0 fl oz per gallon (May/Jun and Sep/Oct rotation) — repellent.
  - Taurus SC at 0.8 fl oz per gallon (Mar/Apr and Jul/Aug rotation) — non-repellent; label maximum 2 applications per year, no application further than 1 ft from the structure.
- **Fipronil Plus-C** at 0.75 fl oz per gallon — exterior SPOT treatment only for roach service calls; not a full perimeter product. Non-repellent: use the dedicated non-repellent sprayer.
- **Niban granular bait** — about 0.5 lb per home in conducive areas (shrub beds, AC drip lines, wet areas, near trash cans), band approximately 4 ft wide. Apply only where no residual spray went down — spray residue contaminates bait.
- **Tekko Pro IGR** at 1 fl oz per gallon — growth regulator to pair with a residual or bait; per label instructions for placement.

## Treatment Steps
1. Inspect the exterior: crawlspace vents, weep holes, mulch beds, wood piles, and drains.
2. De-web and clear harborage debris around the foundation.
3. Apply the rotation residual to the perimeter and entry points per label.
4. Spot-treat active harborages with Fipronil Plus-C (exterior spots only).
5. Spread Niban in conducive areas away from sprayed zones.
6. Treat verified interior activity at entry points only; prefer monitors and baits indoors.

## Customer Prep
- Move firewood and stored items away from the foundation.
- Trim shrubs at least 6 inches off the structure.
- Fix moisture problems: gutters, AC condensation lines, leaky spigots.

## Follow-up
- Recheck in 30 days, then move to the regular quarterly exterior service.
- Free re-service between visits if activity continues, per program terms.

## Safety
- Never layer a repellent over a non-repellent or over Niban bait.
- Do not treat electrical meters, children's play equipment, firewood itself, or near water.
- Wear label-required PPE.
