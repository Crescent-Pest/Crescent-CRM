# Spider Treatment

## Identification
Common Lowcountry spiders: orb weavers and spinybacked orb weavers (webs across porches and shrubs), wolf spiders (ground hunters, no webs), cellar spiders, and occasional widow spiders in protected voids (water meter boxes, cluttered garages). Evidence: webbing on eaves, soffits, and shrubs; egg sacs in web corners; spiders themselves. Coverage: spiders up to 15 feet are included in the standard programs.

## Approach
Mechanical web removal first, then a perimeter residual. Knocking down webs removes egg sacs and forces spiders through treated surfaces; spraying webs just plasters them to the house. Spider pressure follows insect pressure, so the general perimeter treatment does double duty by removing their food.

## Products
- **Webster brush** — knock down all webs and egg sacs up to 15 feet.
- **Seasonal rotation residual** for the perimeter, applied per label (1 ft up and 1 ft out):
  - Barricor at 0.5 fl oz per gallon (Jan/Feb, Nov/Dec) — repellent.
  - Onslaught FastCap at 1.0 fl oz per gallon (May/Jun, Sep/Oct) — repellent; good substitution for stubborn pests.
  - Taurus SC months (Mar/Apr, Jul/Aug): Taurus is ant-focused — for spider-specific visits in those months use a cheat-sheet alternate below rather than adding a third Taurus application.
- **Alternates** (repellents — never over a non-repellent or Niban): Suspend SC at 0.75 fl oz per gallon, Suspend Polyzone at 0.75 fl oz per gallon (cheat sheet also lists 1 fl oz per gallon — unresolved discrepancy, confirm with the Service Manager), Demand CS at 0.4 fl oz per gallon.
- Interior: monitors in garages and basements per label; direct treatment only for verified widow harborage, per label instructions.

## Treatment Steps
1. De-web the entire structure with the Webster brush up to 15 ft: eaves, soffits, window frames, porch ceilings, shrubs against the house.
2. Remove egg sacs — each one prevents dozens of spiderlings.
3. Apply the perimeter residual per label to eaves lines, window and door frames, weep holes, and other web anchor points.
4. Check meter boxes, mailbox posts, and playsets for widow spiders; treat verified harborages per label.
5. Place monitors in the garage corners if interior activity was reported.

## Customer Prep
- Reduce exterior lighting or switch to yellow bug bulbs — lights attract the insects spiders eat.
- Keep shrubs trimmed 6 inches off the structure.
- Knock down stored-item clutter in garages where widows harbor.

## Follow-up
- Webbing rebuilds between visits are normal; re-service is included between quarterly visits if activity is heavy.
- Recheck widow harborage areas at the next visit.

## Safety
- Treat widow spiders with care — do not handle; treat and remove per label.
- Never apply repellent alternates over non-repellent treatments or Niban bait.
- Wear label-required PPE, especially eye protection when working overhead.
