# Rat Treatment

## Identification
Roof rats and Norway rats. Evidence: large droppings (blunt ends for Norway, pointed for roof rats), heavy gnaw marks on wood and plastic, burrows along foundations (Norway), noise and droppings in attics (roof rats), greasy rub marks on beams and pipes. Roof rats dominate in the Charleston area — check attics, trees, and roof lines.

## Approach
Exterior bait station program plus exclusion, same framework as the mouse program but with more attention to roof access. Interior infestation inspections are free for Plus customers; otherwise $175 during business hours / $300 after hours (per current program terms — confirm with the office). Plus customers get 30% off rodent-related service calls and exclusion work.

## Products
- **Protecta Evo Express stations** — exterior perimeter placement by linear footage:
  - 200 linear ft or less: 6 stations (5 Evo Express, 1 Evo Landscape)
  - 200–300 linear ft: 8 stations (6 Evo Express, 2 Evo Landscape)
  - 300–400 linear ft: 12 stations (8 Evo Express, 4 Evo Landscape)
  - Over 400 linear ft: discuss with the Service Manager.
  Conducive-area placement beats even spacing: fence lines, vegetation, AC platforms, burrow areas. Evo Circuit stations along walkways avoid trip hazards.
- **Fasttrac Blox** — 4 blox per station initially; at least 2 for light activity, up to 8 where bait is fully consumed. One feeding is lethal; rodents die in 5–7 days. Secured stations only, per label.
- Attic: snap traps sized for rats, placed on runways per label. No loose bait in attics or crawlspaces.
- Exclusion: hardware cloth for vents, sealant and metal flashing for roof-line gaps, chimney caps — per job scope.

## Treatment Steps
1. Inspect the attic for droppings, nesting, and chewed wiring; photograph findings.
2. Walk the roof line and exterior: overhanging branches, gable vents, soffit gaps, utility entries.
3. Place Protecta Evo stations by the linear-footage table, loaded with 4 Fasttrac Blox each.
4. Set rat-sized snap traps in the attic on runways per label.
5. Quote exclusion for every entry point found — rats re-enter through anything left open.
6. Recommend trimming branches at least 10 ft off the roof (roof-rat highway).

## Customer Prep
- Remove bird feeders or move them well away from the structure during the program.
- Pick up fallen fruit and secure garbage can lids.
- Clear attic access so traps can be placed and checked.

## Follow-up
- Check stations and attic traps in 1–2 weeks; replace consumed, moldy, or deteriorated bait.
- Replace broken or unsecured stations immediately — never leave bait unsecured.
- After activity stops, verify exclusion work sealed the entry points, then move to routine station maintenance.

## Safety
- Fasttrac Blox only in secured, labeled stations — follow all label directions.
- Wear gloves and a respirator when working around rat droppings in enclosed attics or crawlspaces.
- Watch for wiring damage — flag chewed wires to the customer as a fire risk.
