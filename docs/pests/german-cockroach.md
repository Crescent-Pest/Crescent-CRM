# German Cockroach Treatment

## Identification
Small (about 1/2 inch) light-brown roach with two dark stripes behind the head. Found indoors near heat, moisture, and food — kitchens, bathrooms, appliance motors. Evidence: pepper-like droppings in corners and hinges, egg cases (oothecae), and a musty odor in heavy infestations. German roaches are excluded from the Essential and Advanced programs — this is a dedicated cleanout service.

## Approach
Pure non-repellent approach. Roaches walk through treated areas unknowingly and transfer lethal doses back to the harborage. Do NOT use repellent sprays indoors for German roaches — repellents scatter the population and make the cleanout harder.

## Products
- **Alpine WSG** (dinotefuran, non-repellent) — primary interior product. Accusprayer mix: 2.5 g per 16 fl oz water. Backpack power sprayer mix: 20 g per gallon. NOTE: the internal cheat sheet also lists 10 g per gallon for active-infestation cleanouts — this discrepancy is unresolved; confirm the rate with the Service Manager and the product label before mixing.
- **Gentrol IGR** (hydroprene growth regulator) — pair with Alpine WSG to break the reproductive cycle. Accusprayer mix: 0.125 fl oz per 16 fl oz water. Backpack power sprayer mix: 1 fl oz per gallon.
- **Ridesco WG** (non-repellent) — cleanout product for active infestations. Accusprayer mix: 2.5 g per 16 fl oz water. Backpack sprayer mix: 20 g per gallon. Exterior use is exclusive to Ridesco WG for cleanouts. Active-infestation cleanout work must be performed by a certified technician.
- **Niban granular bait** — exterior conducive areas only, per label instructions. Keep away from any sprayed areas.
- Sticky monitors — place in cabinets, under sinks, and behind appliances to track activity between visits.

## Treatment Steps
1. Interview the customer: where roaches are seen, when, and how many.
2. Inspect kitchen and bathrooms with a flashlight: under sinks, behind the refrigerator and stove, dishwasher motor area, cabinet hinges, wall voids.
3. Apply Alpine WSG plus Gentrol IGR to cracks, crevices, and harborage areas found during inspection.
4. For heavy active infestations, a certified technician performs the cleanout (Ridesco WG per the rates above).
5. Place sticky monitors to measure remaining activity.
6. Do not contaminate treated areas with repellent products on this or later visits.

## Customer Prep
- Empty and wipe out cabinets under sinks before the visit.
- Fix drips and leaks; roaches need water more than food.
- Clear clutter (paper bags, cardboard) from kitchen and pantry.
- Keep food sealed and do a nightly wipe-down of counters during the cleanout period.

## Follow-up
- Recheck in 2 weeks; severe infestations may take several visits to get under control.
- Replace monitors and re-treat harborages showing activity.
- Move to regular preventive service once monitors stay clean for a full cycle.

## Safety
- Follow all label directions and wear label-required PPE (gloves, goggles).
- Keep children and pets away from treated surfaces until dry.
